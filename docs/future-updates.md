# What is coming

No dates. The order changes as real usage teaches us what matters.

- A searchnapply plugin for ChatGPT, the same tools in a second ecosystem.
- Email digests of what your monitors found.
- More hiring platforms, LinkedIn postings among them.
- Import your profile from LinkedIn (EU members first, where LinkedIn allows it).
- Market statistics: response rates, demand by role and place, over time.
- A public job-search page, no account needed.
- More resume and cover-letter templates and themes.
- Opening intelligence: reposts and silent closings flagged.
- Company facts pulled in for better cover letters.
